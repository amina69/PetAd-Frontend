# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** [Date]

## Introduction

Welcome to PetAd! We are committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use our pet adoption platform.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Name and contact details**: Provided during registration and profile creation
- **Email address**: Used for account verification and communications
- **Phone number**: Optional, for adoption coordination
- **National Identity Number (NIN)**: Required for user verification

### Pet and Adoption Information
- **Pet profiles**: Names, breeds, ages, descriptions, and photos uploaded by shelters
- **Adoption applications**: Your interests, motivations, and arrangements
- **Custody requests**: Temporary care arrangements and duration

### Technical Information
- **IP address** and device information for security and analytics
- **Browser type and version**
- **Usage patterns** and session duration

## How We Use Your Information

- To facilitate pet adoption and temporary custody arrangements
- To communicate with you about adoption status and updates
- To verify user identities and prevent fraud
- To improve our platform and user experience
- To comply with legal obligations and regulations

## Information Sharing

We may share information with:
- **Shelters and rescue partners** involved in adoption processes
- **Veterinary partners** for health coordination
- **Legal authorities** when required by law
- **Service providers** who assist with platform operations (under confidentiality agreements)

## Your Rights

- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction**: Update inaccurate or incomplete information
- **Deletion**: Request removal of your data (except where retention is required)

## Children's Privacy

Our platform is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
