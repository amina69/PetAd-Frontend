# Cookie Policy

**Last Updated:** [Date]

## Overview

This cookie policy explains how PetAd uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our pet adoption platform. By using our Platform, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information.

## Types of Cookies We Use

### Essential Cookies
- **Necessary for basic functionality**: These cookies are required for the Platform to function properly
- **Authentication**: Remember your login status and session
- **Security**: Enable secure areas of the Platform
- **Cannot be disabled** in our system

### Analytics Cookies
- **Performance analysis**: Understand how visitors interact with our Platform
- **Visitor counting**: Track unique and returning visitors
- **Traffic source analysis**: Determine where visitors come from
- **Performance improvement**: Identify pages needing optimization

### Functionality Cookies
- **Remember preferences**: Store your settings and preferences
- **Personalization**: Customize your experience based on your choices
- **Enhanced features**: Enable optional features and services

### Marketing/Tracking Cookies
- **Advertising**: Deliver relevant ads and measure their effectiveness
- **Cross-site tracking**: Build interest profiles for marketing purposes
- **Conversion tracking**: Measure ad campaign performance
- **May be disabled** via your consent choices

## Your Choices

### Managing Cookies

You can manage cookies through:

1. **Browser settings**: Most browsers allow you to reject or delete cookies
2. **Consent banner**: Use the cookie consent banner on our Platform
3. **Opt-out links**: "Manage cookies" link in the website footer

### Withdrawing Consent

- You can withdraw your consent at any time
- Withdrawing consent may affect certain Platform features
- Essential cookies cannot be disabled as they are required for functionality

## Third-Party Cookies

We may allow selected third parties to set cookies for:
- **Analytics services** (Google Analytics)
- **Marketing platforms** (with your consent)
- **Social media features** (like, share buttons)

## Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly place cookies for children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.
